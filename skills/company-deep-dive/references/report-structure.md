# Company Deep Dive Report Structure

Use this structure to analyze whether a private-market company is a good investment target inside a validated sector thesis.

## 1. Executive Investment View

- Recommendation: `推进投资条款讨论`, `进入强DD`, `补材料后再判断`, `跟踪观察`, or `Pass`.
- One-sentence company thesis.
- One-sentence main concern.
- Whether the company has alpha beyond sector beta.
- What must be true for the investment to work.
- What would make us stop.
- Key next action.

## 2. Source Inventory and Evidence Quality

- Materials reviewed.
- Source date and origin.
- Evidence quality label.
- What each source is useful for.
- What each source cannot prove.

## 3. Company Snapshot

- Company name, founding date, location, financing stage.
- Product/service.
- Target customer.
- Business model.
- Current traction.
- Round amount, valuation, use of proceeds.
- Existing investors.
- Team size and key people.

## 4. Industry Thesis Fit

- Which sector thesis from `brief-industry-research` or `deep-industry-research` applies.
- Where the company sits in the industry value chain.
- Whether it is in a high-value investable segment.
- Whether the company benefits from the key industry inflections.
- Whether industry research strengthens or weakens the company case.

## 5. Technology and Product Analysis

- Product architecture.
- Technical route.
- Key performance metrics.
- Product roadmap.
- Technical proof: demo, benchmark, tape-out, yield, reliability, certification, patents.
- Comparison with substitutes and competitors.
- Technical risks and verification gaps.

Use argument-driven format for key judgments.

## 6. Commercialization and Customer Validation

- Customer list by product.
- Commercialization ladder by customer:
  `sample/prototype -> pilot -> customer validation -> design-in -> design-win/nomination -> mass production -> recognized revenue -> cash collection`.
- Contract status.
- Revenue contribution.
- Repeat purchase or expansion.
- Customer concentration.
- Sales cycle and procurement process.
- Customer quality and referenceability.

## 7. Revenue Quality and Business Model

- Revenue by year/month/product/customer.
- Contracted amount vs recognized revenue vs collection.
- Recurring vs one-time revenue.
- Product revenue vs NRE/development fee vs samples/projects.
- Gross margin and cost structure.
- Unit economics.
- Working capital and receivables.
- Revenue forecast credibility.

## 8. Competitive Landscape and Company Alpha

- Direct competitors.
- Substitute technologies or solutions.
- Customer alternatives.
- Why this company could win.
- Why competitors could win instead.
- Moat: technology, process know-how, customer qualification, patents, data, supply chain, cost, ecosystem, speed.
- Strength of moat today vs projected moat.

## 9. Team and Organization

- Founder-market fit.
- Technical leadership.
- Commercial leadership.
- Manufacturing/operations capability.
- Finance and governance.
- Hiring gaps.
- Incentives, equity, option pool if available.
- Key-person risk.

## 10. Financials, Financing, and Runway

- Historical P&L, cash flow, balance sheet if available.
- Burn rate and runway.
- Use of proceeds.
- Milestones funded by the round.
- Financing history.
- Cap table and liquidation preferences if available.
- Next-round dependency.

## 11. Valuation and Return Analysis

- Proposed pre-money/post-money.
- Revenue multiple, gross profit multiple, or other relevant metrics.
- Comparable company/transaction references.
- Scenario return model: downside/base/upside.
- What valuation assumes.
- Valuation red flags.

## 12. Exit Path

- IPO path.
- M&A path.
- Strategic buyers.
- Public comps.
- What scale and metrics are needed for exit.
- Likely exit timing.

## 13. Key Risks and Bear Case

- Technical risk.
- Commercialization risk.
- Customer concentration risk.
- Financing risk.
- Valuation risk.
- Competition risk.
- Governance/team risk.
- Exit risk.
- Strongest bear case.
- Risk mitigants and deal protections.

## 14. Diligence Priorities

No interview script is required by default, but include what must be verified:

- Documents to request.
- Data tables needed.
- Customer/third-party facts to verify.
- Technical proof needed.
- Financial/contract proof needed.
- Legal/cap table items needed.

## 15. Investment Decision and Conditions

- Final recommendation.
- Required conditions before IC.
- Required conditions before signing.
- Deal structure considerations.
- Follow-up owner and timeline if useful.

## 16. Appendix

- Evidence table.
- Customer commercialization table.
- Competitor comparison.
- Financial summary.
- Source links and local file references.
