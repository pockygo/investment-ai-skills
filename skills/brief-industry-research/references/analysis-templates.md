# Brief Industry Analysis Templates

Use only the templates that answer a real screening question. Merge columns when evidence is sparse. Never invent data to complete a table.

## 1. Technology Route Selection

| Route | Customer value | Key performance metric | Cost | Maturity | Adoption evidence | Substitute risk | Investment view |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|

Use when several technologies can solve the same customer problem. Compare the metrics customers actually use, not generic feature lists.

## 2. Industry Stage and Inflection

| Time or stage | Technology state | Customer behavior | Supply structure | Trigger event | Positive verification signal | Investment implication |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|

Use to distinguish a current fact from a future catalyst. Do not call a year the “last window” without a falsifiable trigger.

## 3. Commercialization Evidence Ladder

| Claim or application | Current stage | Concrete evidence | Buyer and budget | Next milestone | Main failure mode | Confidence |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|

Use the stages: lab, prototype, pilot, validation, design-in, design-win, mass production, recognized revenue, cash collection.

## 4. Value Chain and Profit Pool

| Layer | Product/service | Representative players | Gross-margin or pricing signal | Bargaining power | Capex/working capital | Bottleneck | Investability |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|

Use to show where economic value is captured, not merely to list upstream and downstream names.

## 5. Market Scenario Sanity Check

| Scenario | Customer/adoption assumption | Volume | Unit price | Implied market | Timing | Source/confidence |
|:---|:---|---:|---:|---:|:---|:---|

Show formulas and units. Separate current recognized demand from forecasts.

## 6. Competitive and Startup Window

| Player/archetype | Route and segment | Customer proof | Scale | Structural advantage | Weakness | Startup entry window | Evidence quality |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|

Use comparable companies only when exposure to the same niche is real.

## 7. Trigger-Company Fit and Gap

| Industry success factor | Why it matters | Trigger-company claim/status | Independent proof | Gap | Next diligence action |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|

Use this to connect industry research to the project without performing full company diligence.

## Table Quality Rules

- Use tables for comparison, sequencing, calculation, ranking, or contradiction.
- Prefer 4–8 rows and 4–8 useful columns.
- Write cells with specific mechanisms or evidence; avoid tables made only of `高/中/低`.
- Add a short conclusion below each decisive table.
- Cite the source in the relevant cell, caption, or adjacent note.
- Use prose instead when a table does not make the decision easier.
